Remember this

FintechSoftware and other modern technologies used by businesses that provide automated and improved financial services.
Why it grew

Technology, and the poor reputation of traditional retail banks.

Challenger banks

Small, often new banks using technology for a lower cost base (often no branches). Fully licensed, with depositor protection.

Standalone banking apps

Limited services such as payments. Not fully licensed banks, though they may need electronic money licences.

Examples

Crowd funding, peer-to-peer lending, online currency conversion, digital wallets.

About this simulation

A simplified teaching model. Bikash, Anita, Everest Fashion, Sajilo Bank and PayEasy are illustrative. All fees, rates, times and amounts are made up to show the idea and vary in real life.
