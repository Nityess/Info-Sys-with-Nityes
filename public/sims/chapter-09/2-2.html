Remember this

Internet of things · definitionThe increasing number of devices that send data over the internet to a data processor, where the information is processed.
Sensors on machines

Send performance data to a monitor in real time.

RFID tags

A chip with a unique number, so a product's location can be tracked.

Smart meters and devices

Read and adjusted remotely over the internet.

Car black boxes

Monitor driving and send the data to insurers.

Smartphones

Capture location and send it to apps and services.

Spot it: a device collects data, sends it over the internet, and something is processed or adjusted as a result. A home thermostat that an app adjusts using weather data is the internet of things.
About this simulation

A simplified teaching model. Himal Textiles Pvt Ltd is a made-up business based on the study note's textile maker case; Sujan, Loom 3, the readings, limits, times, places and the device examples' figures are illustrative. Real factories add gateways, local controllers and safety cut-outs that work even without the internet.
