Remember this

Data analyticsThe process of using fields within the source data itself, rather than predetermined formats, to collect, organise and analyse large sets of data to discover patterns and other useful information which an organisation can use for its future business decisions.
Data miningThe process of sorting through data to identify patterns and relationships between different items. It turns raw data into useful information. Predictive analytics is a type of data mining that aims to predict future events.
Built from the fields

Group, filter and search any field you like. Not a fixed report.

Shown visually

Filtered, sorted, highlighted and shown as bar or pie charts.

Fast and useful

Helps with pricing, new products and better decisions. It is not slow.

About this simulation

A simplified teaching model. Everest Fashion Ltd is a made-up business based on the study note's fashion retailer case. The 240 orders, prices, stores and patterns are generated for teaching and are illustrative. The forecast is a straight line through three months; real predictive models use far more data.
