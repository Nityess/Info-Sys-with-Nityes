Remember this

Distributed ledger technology (DLT)Technology that allows people who do not know each other to trust a shared record of events.
No central store

Replicated, synchronised data is shared across the whole network, however spread out. Everyone has equal access to the same information.

What the record gives

Clarity about ownership of assets, proof that obligations exist, and an easier transfer of ownership.

Real example: Everledger

Records each diamond's unique specification and every trade, so ownership and history can be traced and fraud prevented.

DLT and blockchain

Blockchain is a process used in many distributed ledgers to make sure only valid transactions are recorded (topic 2.5).
