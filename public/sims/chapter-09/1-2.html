    Remember this

    GroupTypeWhat it isExample
    StructuredCreatedDeliberately created by an organisation for product or market research.Customers logging into a retailer's account; RFID tags on inventory.
    ProvokedData from people given the opportunity to express their views.Ratings and reviews on a retailer's website.
    TransactedData about actual transactions, including all steps and website traffic leading up to each transaction.Online purchase journey including pages visited and cart additions.
    CompiledCollected by a third party (market research, credit rating or polling organisation) and accessed by a business.Credit reference data.
    UnstructuredCapturedCreated passively from unrelated activity, captured without a specific purpose.Smartphone location data; search engine records of which sites were accessed.
    User-generatedData that internet users create and voluntarily place online.Tweets, photos, blog posts, forum reviews.
    Spot it: ask who wanted the data. A review on the retailer's own site is provoked (the retailer asked). A review someone posts on a public forum is user-generated. Data a credit agency sells you is compiled.
