Remember this

Cognitive technologiesTechnologies involving machines that can analyse data to extract patterns and meaning, derive new information, and identify strategies and behaviours to act on the results of their analysis.
Machine learningA form of AI which uses computer algorithms that improve automatically through experience using very large data sets. Algorithms build a mathematical model to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed, and adaptively improve their performance as training data increases.
Robotic process automation (RPA)A combination of process automation and machine learning so that robots can learn how to do manual tasks by observing humans at work.
Artificial intelligenceThe use of computers to do tasks which are thought to require human intelligence, such as learning, knowing, sensing, reasoning, creating things, problem-solving, and generating and understanding language.
Own rules, not given rules

Machine learning builds its own rules from data. Pre-programmed rules are not machine learning.

A subset of AI

Machine learning is one form of artificial intelligence.

Predictive vs prescriptive

Predictive: what will happen. Prescriptive: what to do to reach the goal.

RPA needs routine

Repetitive tasks with little creativity or judgement. Exceptions go to people.

Intuition vs reasoning

People decide by intuition (quick, from experience) or reasoning (slower, by logic).

About this simulation

A simplified teaching model. Everest Fashion Ltd and Himal Textiles Pvt Ltd are made-up businesses based on the study note's cases. Ramesh, Suman, the orders, return rates, prices, invoices, suppliers and times are illustrative. The "model" is a small linear regression refitted in your browser; real machine learning uses far larger data and more complex models.
