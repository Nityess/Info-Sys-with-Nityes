Volume. The amount of accessible data is vast. It is available far more easily than even a few years ago.

10 · one quiet shop600 · peak season
What to noticeMore records arrive, the dots crowd the pipes and the engine needs more servers. With very little data the bars jump about; with lots of data they settle. Watch Processing demand pass what one ordinary database server could handle.

Variety. Data is available about a huge variety of issues. Because it comes from many sources it is largely unstructured and must be analysed before use.

What to noticeEach source sees winter demand from one angle and is slightly wrong on its own. Turn sources off until only the till is left, then press Reveal actual winter sales. Add them back one at a time and watch the bars close the gap. Text and images make the engine work harder because they must be analysed first.

Velocity. Data is streamed into the business at great speed. Sales, inventory and other data are available in real time, with no need to wait for periodic, summarised reports.

What to noticeIn nightly mode, data piles up in the amber tray and the recommendation only moves at 00:00. Trigger the cold snap in each mode. Live streaming moves the mix toward jackets within seconds; the nightly report leaves Anita deciding on yesterday's picture.

Veracity. Relates to the trustworthiness and accuracy of big data. All datasets contain inaccuracies, bias, anomalies and noise. Dirty data must be cleaned before it can be relied upon.

What to noticeRed striped dots are dirty data: five-star scarf reviews written by one account and till receipts sent twice. Pin the mix first. Inject the bad data and watch scarves and knitwear swell and trust fall. Then clean it: the filter at the engine door moves bad records to the bin and the mix recovers.

Key points to remember

Big data · McKinseyDatasets whose size is beyond the ability of typical database software to capture, store, manage and analyse.
Big data · GartnerHigh-volume, high-velocity and high-variety information assets that demand cost-effective, innovative forms of information processes for enhanced insight and decision making.
Volume

The amount of accessible data is vast.

In the simulator: more transactions, more storage, more servers.
Velocity

Data is streamed in at great speed and is available in real time.

Real-time streaming is velocity, not variety.
Variety

Data about a huge variety of issues from many sources, largely unstructured.

A wide range of sources and areas is variety.
Veracity

Trustworthiness and accuracy. Dirty data must be cleaned.

Errors, anomalies and misinterpretation are veracity, not velocity.
About this simulation
  • It is a simplified teaching model. Everest Fashion Ltd is a made-up business based on the study note's fashion retailer case; Anita, Nisha, all figures, prices, store data and the website address are illustrative.
  • Each source is given a small built-in bias so that combining sources visibly improves the estimate. Real data sources are not biased in such tidy ways.
  • Processing demand, storage and server counts are shown on a simple scale to make volume visible. They are not real capacity figures.
  • A real analytics system cleans data with many more checks than the two shown here.
