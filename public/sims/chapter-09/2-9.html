Remember this

Shared service centre (SSC)Consolidates the transaction-processing activities of many operations within a company, such as HR, payroll, accounting and IT. The aim is significant cost reductions while improving service levels through standardised technology, processes and service level agreements.
AdvantagesDisadvantages
Reduced headcount from economies of scale in one location.Loss of business-specific knowledge about individual departments.
Lower premises and other overhead costs.Removed from decision making, so less able to give meaningful financial information.
Knowledge sharing improves the quality of service.Weakened relationships because of distance from the business areas.
Standard approaches give more consistent management of business data.Cost inefficiencies may be passed on to divisions, causing friction.
