Remember this

Digital assetAny text or media file that is formatted into a binary source and that includes the right to use it. Digital files that do not carry this right are not considered digital assets.
Two types

Media assets (images, multimedia) and textual content (documents, websites, graphics).

They last

Digital assets continue to exist as technology progresses, whatever device they sit on.

Keep them safe

Unless meant to be free: encoding, encryption or watermarks, carefully worded licences, and clear copyright or IP ownership.
