Remember this

Crypto currenciesDigital tokens that allow users to trade with each other online. An alternative to traditional currencies.
No third party

Paying in crypto does not involve a bank or payment company. In that way it is like paying cash: lower transaction costs and more privacy.

Not the same as cash

It has no physical form, so it cannot be used in the same way as traditional physical currency.

RiskExplanation
VolatilityThe price is subject to large swings.
HackingOnline digital wallets where the currency is stored can be hacked.
Intrinsic value uncertaintyIt is hard to say what it is really worth. Its value comes from the market.
Criminal useThe privacy of payments could help criminals avoid money laundering rules.
About this simulation

A simplified teaching model. Everest Fashion, Bikash and Ramesh are illustrative; the coin, prices, fees and times are made up. Real crypto networks do charge network fees and take time to confirm.
