Remember this

BenefitWhat it means
Enhancing transparencyCreates insights into issues not previously fully understood, such as customer buying patterns or market price fluctuations.
Performance improvementReal-time, analysed information allows managers to make better decisions, resulting in better profitability.
Market segmentation and customisationInsights into customer needs allow the business to tailor product designs and prices to particular customers or groups.
Decision makingReal-time information allows better day-to-day decisions, for instance on pricing or stocking of goods.
InnovationAnalysed big data reveals new ideas, resulting in new products and services. It also underlies the growth of virtual ('borderless') organisations.
Risk managementData analytics can use big data to help identify, quantify and manage risk.
The trap

Faster processing of high-volume routine transactions is what a transaction processing system does. It is not a benefit of big data.
