Remember this

RiskExplanation
StorageThe sheer volume of big data means organisations must monitor and flex storage levels to avoid running out.
Workforce skillsOrganisations need enough data scientists and analysts with the right mix of knowledge and experience.
Data dependencyOrganisations can become dependent on data to make decisions, risking poor decisions if data has errors or is misinterpreted. Links to veracity.
Information overloadHuge numbers of insights can actually slow down business decisions.
Data privacyCollecting, storing and analysing data risks breaching data privacy law. Organisations must comply.
Data securityBig data is a source of competitive advantage, so it must be protected from cybersecurity risks.
Dependency vs security

Trusting wrong data and deciding badly is data dependency. Data being stolen is data security.

Veracity

Misreading or wrong data, like the jam reviews, is a veracity problem.
