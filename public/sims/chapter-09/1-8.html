Remember this

Intelligent systemsComputer-based systems that represent, reason about and interpret data, learning about its structure, analysing it to extract patterns and meaning, deriving new information, and identifying strategies and behaviours to act on the results.
Artificial intelligenceThe use of computers to do tasks which are thought to require human intelligence, such as learning, knowing, sensing, reasoning, creating things, problem-solving, and generating and understanding language.
AutomationThe creation of technology and its application to control and monitor the production and delivery of goods and services, performing tasks previously decided on and performed by humans.
Type of AIIntelligence usedKey characteristic
AnalyticalCognitiveRepresents the world; learns from past experience to inform future decisions.
Human-inspiredCognitive + emotionalUnderstands human emotions as well as cognitive elements.
HumanisedCognitive + emotional + socialThe highest form. Self-conscious and self-aware when interacting with humans.
