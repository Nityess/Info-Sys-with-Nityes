Remember this

SourceDescriptionExamples
Processed dataFrom databases held by traditional businesses and other organisations.Internal financial systems, ERP databases.
Open dataRelease of large amounts of primarily public sector data.Geo-spatial data, transport data, government financial data, public service data.
Human-sourced dataFrom social networks, blogs, emails, text messages and internet searches.Twitter feeds, customer reviews, WhatsApp messages.
Machine-generated dataFrom the internet of things (fixed and mobile sensors) and from computer and website logs.RFID tags, smart meters, car black boxes, website click logs.
About this simulation

A simplified teaching model. Everest Fashion Ltd is a made-up business based on the study note's fashion retailer case. Sujan, Bikash, the street, the government portal and every data sample are illustrative. The four sources and their examples follow the study note.
